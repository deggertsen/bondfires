Your group chat has 400 unread messages and zero actual conversation. Bondfires is where your group keeps talking between meetups, with short videos instead of text, on each person's own schedule.

Record a video when you have a minute. Your people watch and reply when they have theirs. No scheduling, no calls, no endless scroll.

HOW IT WORKS
• Spark a Bondfire: record a short video about what's on your mind and share it with your camp or hearth.
• Your people respond: each reply is another face, recorded when it suits them, stacked under the spark.
• The conversation keeps going: threads, not feeds, so a conversation stays a conversation.

BUILT FOR GROUPS THAT ALREADY EXIST
• The group that meets once a month: book club, small group, running crew, the Thursday breakfast.
• Friends in different cities who haven't heard each other's voices in months.
• Family, in a hearth: a private space for siblings, parents, and kids who moved out.
• Faith, recovery, and mastermind groups that want to stay connected between meetings.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Your fires at the top of Home: a copper ring means new responses, and a gold dot means your spark is waiting for its first answer.
• Automatic captions, summaries, and tags so you can skim a thread or follow along with the sound off.
• Emoji reactions for when you can't record yet: let them know it landed, then answer properly later.
• Camps for your whole group, public or private, with guidelines and member approval.
• Notifications when someone answers, so nothing gets lost.

FREE TO BE PART OF THE GROUP
Joining, watching, and responding are free. Plus, Premium, and Pro let you spark your own Bondfires, run a hearth for the people closest to you, record for longer, and create and lead camps for your group. Prices are shown in the app in your local currency.

Bondfires isn't a feed of strangers. It's the people you already have, showing up more often.
